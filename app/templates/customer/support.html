Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 7-day doorstep return process work? expand_more

You can request a return directly from your Account > Returns section within 7 days of package delivery. Once approved by the seller, our courier will pick up the item and your refund will be credited to your original payment method.

Are products sold by verified sellers authentic? expand_more

Yes. Every vendor on Nova Mart undergoes mandatory GSTIN verification, PAN validation, and authentic supply-chain background screening before their products are published.

What are the shipping delivery timeframes? expand_more

Metro cities receive delivery in 24 to 48 hours via Delhivery or Blue Dart. Regional locations typically take 3 to 5 business days. Real-time courier waybill tracking is available in your account.

How do I contact an individual seller? expand_more

You can view any seller's store profile, feedback rating, and address by clicking on their store tag on any product detail page.
